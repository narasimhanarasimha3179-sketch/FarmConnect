# 🎨 FarmConnect — UI/UX Structure & Screen Hierarchy

## 1. Design System Foundations

* **Primary Palette:**
  * Forest Green (`#1b5e20`): Brand identity, headers, confirmation states.
  * Leaf Green (`#2e7d32`): Primary call-to-actions, buttons, accent indicators.
  * Light Mint (`#e8f5e9`): Card backgrounds, status pill fills, neutral highlights.
* **Alert & Diagnostic Colors:**
  * Severe / Loss (`#c62828`): High infection severity, price drops, validation errors.
  * Warning (`#f57c00`): Moderate disease alert, pending bid approval.
  * Healthy / Growth (`#388e3c`): Healthy foliage, positive mandi trends.
* **Typography:** System Sans-Serif (`Roboto` on Android, `Inter / Segoe UI` on Web) with minimum 16px font sizing for high legibility in direct sunlight.
